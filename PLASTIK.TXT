To run this demo, please:
Have a soundcard.
Have a P200 or better.

DOS version:
VESA 2.0 With linear framebuffer in:
640x4x0x16 bit
use -? for options

Press [SPACE] for a screenshot. (saved as .bmp)
Press [SHIFT] for fps rate.

Windows version:
Win95/WinNT
DirectX 3+ (Included in WinNT SP3)
Ability to run 640x4x0 in 16bit or 15bit colours.
Use F12 at your own risk.
Lower midasbuffer for better timing
enable interpolation for better sound quality!
